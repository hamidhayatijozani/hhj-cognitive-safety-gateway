# Sbat Product Definition

Sbat is an evidence-bound decision intelligence layer for AI agents and autonomous workflows.

## Buyer problem

AI systems can produce actions that are locally plausible but insufficiently evidenced, policy-incompatible, poorly attributable, or difficult to reconstruct after the fact. Sbat creates an independent assessment boundary around those decisions.

## Product loop

Proposal -> Evidence -> Risk/Policy Evaluation -> Assessment -> Evidence Ledger -> Assurance Report.

## Product modules

1. Decision Evaluator
2. Cognitive Risk Engine
3. Red-Team Scenario Engine
4. Multi-Agent Evaluation
5. Evidence Ledger
6. Assurance Report
7. Python SDK / CLI

## What is measured

Evidence coverage, evidence reliability, policy compliance, deterministic risk signals, verdict stability under challenge, agent agreement, and cryptographic record integrity.

## What is not claimed

Sbat does not claim zero hallucinations, legal admissibility, liability elimination, perfect risk prediction, or guaranteed business outcomes.

## Commercial packaging

Pilot: isolated deployment and scenario assessment.
Team: SDK, API, policy management and evidence storage.
Enterprise: tenant isolation, SSO/RBAC, retention controls, audit export, managed deployment and support.

Pricing is intentionally not hard-coded until customer validation establishes willingness to pay.
